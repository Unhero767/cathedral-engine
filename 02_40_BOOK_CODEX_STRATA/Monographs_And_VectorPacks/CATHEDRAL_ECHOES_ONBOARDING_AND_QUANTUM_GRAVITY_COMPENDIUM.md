## III. DEEP DIVE: THE FIVE PARADIGMS

### 1. Loop Quantum Gravity & Spin Foams (LQG)
* **Foundational Mechanics**: General relativity is cast into Hamiltonian form using Ashtekar-Barbero variables $(A_a^i, E_i^a)$. Space is not a continuous manifold but a discrete quantum network consisting of 1D holonomy loops and nodes (spin networks). Spatial volume and surface area are quantized operators possessing discrete eigenvalues.
* **Recent Advances (2021–2026)**:
  - *Black Hole Singularity Resolution*: Covariant spin foam models have proven that the classical singularity at $r=0$ in Schwarzschild and Kerr black holes is replaced by a high-curvature quantum transition region (a "white hole bounce"), releasing trapped information without violating unitarity.
  - *Phenomenology in Loop Quantum Cosmology (LQC)*: High-precision cosmological datasets have tested quantum bounce tensor-to-scalar perturbation ratios against CMB B-mode limits.
* **RPG Mechanics**:
  - **Inherent Trait**: *Polymer Geometry*. Gain +2 Armor against continuous field and area-of-effect damage.
  - **Signature Move**: *Holonomy Sever*. Spend 1 Momentum to untie a node in the local spin network, removing an enemy's movement action for 1 turn.

### 2. Holographic Duality & Quantum Information ("It from Qubit")
* **Foundational Mechanics**: Gravitational dynamics in an $(d+1)$-dimensional bulk anti-de Sitter (AdS) spacetime are mathematically equivalent to a conformal field theory (CFT) without gravity living on the $d$-dimensional boundary. Spacetime geometry emerges directly from the quantum entanglement of boundary qubits via the Ryu-Takayanagi formula.
* **Recent Advances (2021–2026)**:
  - *The Page Curve & Quantum Extremal Islands*: Gravitational path integrals including replica wormholes have derived the unitary Page curve for black hole evaporation, proving that information escapes via quantum extremal surfaces.
  - *Wormhole Teleportation*: Experimental realization of holographic teleportation protocols on quantum processor hardware (SYK model duality), validating the $ER=EPR$ conjecture (Einstein-Rosen bridges = Einstein-Podolsky-Rosen entanglement).
* **RPG Mechanics**:
  - **Inherent Trait**: *Boundary Projection*. Can project actions across the room or behind barriers by calculating the boundary entanglement surface.
  - **Signature Move**: *ER=EPR Teleport*. When an ally suffers damage, immediately swap places with them through a micro-wormhole and clear 1 Paradox.

### 3. Causal Dynamical Triangulations (CDT) & Causal Sets
* **Foundational Mechanics**: Spacetime is built by summing over all possible causal triangulations of 4-simplices glued together along space-like and time-like faces. Unlike unconstrained Euclidean quantum gravity, CDT imposes strict Lorentzian causality (a global time foliation), preventing unphysical acausal geometries.
* **Recent Advances (2021–2026)**:
  - *Dynamic Dimensional Reduction*: Numerical simulations prove that while spacetime is 4-dimensional on macroscopic scales, its spectral dimension smoothly runs down to $d_s \approx 2$ at the Planck scale.
  - *Quantum Ricci Curvature*: The development of coordinate-independent quantum Ricci curvature has allowed physicists to observe spontaneous emergence of background de Sitter-like geometries from pure quantum fluctuations without fine-tuning.
* **RPG Mechanics**:
  - **Inherent Trait**: *Causal Primacy*. Always acts first in initiative order. Immune to timeline altering or paradox-based retroactive attacks.
  - **Signature Move**: *Foliation Lock*. Freeze an environmental hazard or incoming attack in a static time-slice for 1 round.

### 4. Asymptotic Safety & Metric-Affine Gravity
* **Foundational Mechanics**: Gravity may be a fundamental quantum field theory that avoids non-renormalizability not by introducing new particles (like strings), but through the existence of a non-trivial Ultraviolet (UV) Fixed Point in the Renormalization Group (RG) flow governed by the Wetterich Functional RG equation.
* **Recent Advances (2021–2026)**:
  - *Standard Model Compatibility*: Non-perturbative calculations have verified that the gravitational UV fixed point remains stable and predictive even when coupled to all known Standard Model gauge fields, quarks, and Higgs sectors.
  - *Horizon Physics*: Derivation of scale-dependent black hole metrics with regular horizons that do not suffer from thermodynamic instability.
* **RPG Mechanics**:
  - **Inherent Trait**: *Scale Invariance*. When subjected to extreme, lethal damage, the character's defenses scale asymptotically, capping maximum damage taken in a single blow to 3.
  - **Signature Move**: *Renormalization Surge*. Convert incoming hostile kinetic energy directly into a +2 bonus on the next roll.
